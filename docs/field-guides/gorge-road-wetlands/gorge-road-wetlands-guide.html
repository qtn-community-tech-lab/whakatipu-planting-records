Gorge Rd Wetlands
Plants to look for

Four species are linked to the wetlands subarea in the allocation records: 35 recorded plants, all from Spring 2016. These are a shrub, a sedge, a grass and flax. The photographs below are reference examples from Wikipedia / Wikimedia, taken elsewhere.

Location still needs confirmation. All four records carry site ID 5 (Gorge Rd Wetlands), but the spreadsheet header says “GORGE QPS”. WRT has flagged the inferred location as uncertain, with low confidence. These are allocation records, not verified plantings or a current survival survey. Carex secta also has an unresolved species question involving Carex maorica.

Pūrei / pukio

Carex secta · 13 recorded

Look for a dense clump of narrow, drooping leaves. Older plants can develop a raised, trunk-like base.

Olearia bullata

Olearia bullata · 8 recorded

A small shrub with many interlacing twigs and small brownish-green leaves, growing to about 3 metres.

Toetoe

Austroderia richardii · 7 recorded

Look for a large grass clump with long leaves and tall, arching flower stems bearing creamy, drooping plumes.

Phormium tenax · 7 recorded

How to repeat this lookup

  1. Open the local project, then select Gorge Road area under the sites list.
  2. On the site page, find Parts of this site → Gorge Rd Wetlands. It reports 35 plants in Spring 2016, all needing review. The subarea entry currently has no navigation link.
  3. Open All records. The tested Wetlands search URL retains the search in the address, but returned all 322 Gorge Road records / 4,657 plants. Do not treat it as a working subarea filter.
  4. Set Year → 2016 and Planting season → Spring, then Apply. Bookmark this season view. It shows 40 records / 390 plants across the wider Gorge Road area.
  5. In the detailed Records table, open Source for the four rows below. Confirm the ruling mentions ID 5 (Gorge Rd Wetlands). Keep their location uncertainty attached when reusing the list.
