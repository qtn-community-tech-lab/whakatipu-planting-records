## How these were produced

Five queries, each a filter and a sum over one table. In Google Sheets the same answers are a `QUERY` or pivot over a `Planting` tab with a site-name lookup; in a database they are `GROUP BY`. The point is that none needs an old workbook, a column letter or a species row number.
